### Project Description

**Pinneyavaam** is an intentionally anti-productive operating system designed to help you do absolutely anything except what you actually need to do.

It comes with a gravity-powered cursor, a Flappy Bird that refuses to let you lose, an alarm that believes in infinite snoozing, and an AI chatbot powered by Gemini that gives you surprisingly convincing reasons to procrastinate.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)

Modern technology has become dangerously productive.

People are finishing assignments, waking up on time, completing tasks, and actually getting things done.

We felt this was getting out of hand.

So we identified a serious problem:

> **People are not procrastinating enough.**

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)

Introducing **Pinneyavaam** — an operating system built around one simple philosophy:

> **Why do it now when you can do it later?**

Pinneyavaam turns ordinary computer interactions into completely unnecessary experiences.

* 🖱️ **Gravity Cursor** — Your cursor gets heavier the further down you move it.
* 🐦 **Helpful Flappy Bird** — The game secretly moves pipes out of your way so you don't lose.
* ⏰ **Anti-Productivity Alarm** — Wake-up is optional. Snoozing is encouraged.
* 🤖 **UselessBot** — A Gemini-powered AI assistant that gives you creative and convincing excuses to avoid your work.
* 📊 **Procrastination Stats** — Because apparently we need analytics for wasting time.
* 👁️ **Eye Contact Tracker** — Maintain eye contact with your screen, prove that you can focus, and get rewarded by being released into random dopamine-inducing content.

The more time you waste, the more successful Pinneyavaam considers you.
